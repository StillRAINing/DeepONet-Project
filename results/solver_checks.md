The violent sample is not in the asymptotic regime for h >= 10 ms (orders 0.5 and 1.3: the trajectory is wrong, not just inaccurate), then shows orders of about 5 between 10 ms and 0.3 ms: faster than 4 because the leading h^4 term is still being overtaken by fast-shrinking higher-order terms. 4 is the asymptotic order; the test in `tests/test_solvers.py` therefore uses the calm sample. At the dataset step (2.5 ms) the violent sample's error is about 1e-2, the calm one's about 1e-6 (max over states and times).

Largest kinetic energy in this motion: 19.6 J, so the drift at the dataset step is a relative 2e-06. RK4 is not symplectic, so the drift is small but not zero. Per halving of h it falls by 35x, 19x, 29x: at least the 16x of a 4th-order method, but not a clean 16 (the drift is the maximum of an oscillating error, so cancellation makes the ratio uneven).
